# Performance Review Checklist

- Performance sheet is actionable for storyboard and video prompt stages.
- 正式包含 `character_performance_profiles`、`beat_performance_notes`、`action_continuity_chains`、`emotion_continuity_chains`、`continuity_rules`、`storyboard_handoff`、`risk_notes`、`next_action`。
- `character_performance_profiles` 写清视线、重心/姿态、手部或道具行为，不是泛泛性格词。
- Major beats contain playable acting detail, not only emotion labels.
- `beat_performance_notes` 至少覆盖多个 beat，并写出 pause/hold、micro-expression、reaction timing 或 blocking 细节。
- `action_continuity_chains`、`emotion_continuity_chains` 说明跨 beat 的承接，而不是孤立描述。
- Gaze, posture, gesture, pause, and prop handling continuity are documented where relevant.
- `storyboard_handoff` 能指导下游决定镜头焦点、reaction shot、blocking 或道具连续性。
- The output helps downstream stages decide blocking and reaction timing without inventing new acting logic.
